# Initial D

> A Japanese street-racing story about mountain-pass (touge) racing, drifting, and a tofu delivery boy in a Toyota AE86.

**Creator:** Shuichi Shigeno | **Genre:** Racing, seinen | **Setting:** Gunma Prefecture, Japan

## Synopsis

Takumi Fujiwara is a high school student who has delivered tofu for his father's shop every morning over Mount Akina since before he could legally drive. Without realizing it, he has become an extraordinarily skilled mountain-pass driver. When local street racers challenge the mysterious "Akina ghost" in a Toyota Sprint Trueno AE86, Takumi is pulled into the competitive world of touge racing. Despite driving an underpowered car, he beats faster machines through technique and skill, facing one rival team after another.

## Main Characters

- **Takumi Fujiwara**: Protagonist. Quiet and unambitious at first, but gifted behind the wheel. Drives his father's AE86.
- **Bunta Fujiwara**: Takumi's father and owner of Fujiwara Tofu Shop. A secretive former racing legend who trained Takumi indirectly.
- **Itsuki Takeuchi**: Takumi's best friend and a car enthusiast who works at a gas station.
- **Natsuki Mogi**: Takumi's classmate and love interest.
- **Ryosuke Takahashi**: Leader of the Akagi RedSuns, the "White Comet of Akagi". A calculating strategist who drives a Mazda FC3S RX-7.
- **Keisuke Takahashi**: Ryosuke's hot-headed younger brother, drives a Mazda FD3S RX-7.
- **Shingo Nakazato**: Rival from the Myogi NightKids.
- **Kyoichi Sudo**: Leader of Team Emperor, drives a Mitsubishi Lancer Evolution.
